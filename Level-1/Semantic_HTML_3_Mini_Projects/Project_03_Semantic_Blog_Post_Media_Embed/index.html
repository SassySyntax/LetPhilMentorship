Gestalt Laws for Web Design

The Gestalt Laws (or principles) describe how the human brain automatically groups visual elements, recognize patterns, and percieve complex structures as a unified whole rather than isolated parts.

  • Proximity - Elements physically close to one another are percieved as a group
  • Similarity - Elements that share visual characteristics (color, shape, etc) are percieved as a group
  • Closure - Elements that look broken are still percieved as one, since the brain tries to fill in gaps
  • Continuity - Elements arranged on a line or smooth curve are percieve as a group
  • Figure Ground - The brain separates objects in the foreground and background
  • Symmetry and Order - The brain prefers simple, balanced shapes
